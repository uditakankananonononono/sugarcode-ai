# Cell-Twin

Cell-Twin: A digital twin engine that creates virtual clones of patient cells using multi-omic data. It runs "in silico" drug trials to predict responses, including apoptosis and proliferation rates. Cell-Twin enables personalized medicine by testing therapeutic combinations on digital surrogate cells, identifying the most effective treatments for individual patients.
