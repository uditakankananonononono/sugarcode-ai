# Microbiome Exp

Microbiome Exp: A taxonomic analysis engine for 16S rRNA data, providing diversity indices, taxonomic distributions, and functional potential summaries. It correlates microbial profiles with publication trends and disease states, offering a comprehensive view of interactions. Microbiome Exp provides the data necessary for understanding the microbiome's role in human health.
