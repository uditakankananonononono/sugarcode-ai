# PDX Insight

PDX Insight: A monitoring tool for Patient-Derived Xenografts that ensures they accurately represent patient biology over time. It calculates a "Model Fidelity Index" and identifies translational drift using multi-omic data. PDX Insight suggests CRISPR-based restoration strategies to maintain model accuracy, improving the reliability of preclinical drug testing.
