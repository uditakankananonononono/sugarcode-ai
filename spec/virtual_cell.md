# Virtual Cell

Virtual Cell: A multi-scale simulator that integrates metabolic flux models with gene regulatory networks. It predicts cell growth, protein expression, and phenotypic responses to environmental changes or genetic perturbations. Virtual Cell provides a comprehensive digital environment for testing hypotheses, enabling researchers to explore system-wide behaviors and optimize cellular performance.
