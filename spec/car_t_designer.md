# CAR-T Designer

CAR-T Designer: An optimization platform for chimeric antigen receptor constructs, suggesting scFv domains and signaling elements for specific cancer antigens. It predicts potential toxicities and simulates clinical trial outcomes. CAR-T Designer enables the engineering of safe and effective immune cell therapies, accelerating the development of personalized immunotherapies for cancer.
