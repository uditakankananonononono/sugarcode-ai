# CellPainter

CellPainter: A visualization tool for multiplex fluorescent imaging signatures, mapping cellular morphological changes in response to drugs or mutations. It identifies unique staining patterns associated with specific biological perturbations. CellPainter enables the discovery of novel drug mechanisms and the characterization of phenotypes by comparing image data across experimental conditions.
