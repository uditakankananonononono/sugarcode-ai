# Fate Predictor

Fate Predictor: A tool that predicts the transcription factors and protocols required for direct cellular reprogramming. It maps the trajectory between cell types, such as turning skin cells into functional neurons. Fate Predictor provides success rate estimates and protocol summaries, enabling researchers to design strategies for regenerative medicine and disease.
