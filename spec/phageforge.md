# PhageForge

PhageForge: A specialized toolkit for engineering bacteriophages equipped with CRISPR payloads to target multi-drug resistant pathogens. It modifies phage genomes to improve host targeting and delivery efficiency. PhageForge enables the design of highly specific antimicrobial therapies that selectively eliminate harmful bacteria while preserving the beneficial microbes within biological communities.
