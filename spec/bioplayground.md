# BioPlayground

BioPlayground: An evolutionary sandbox for introducing selective pressures and monitoring population drift in engineered systems. it allows researchers to test the robustness of synthetic organisms under diverse environmental conditions. BioPlayground provides a platform for directed evolution and ecological modeling sketches, letting users explore how construct frequencies change under selection, drift, and mutation.
