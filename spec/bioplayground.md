# BioPlayground

BioPlayground: An evolutionary sandbox for introducing selective pressures and monitoring population drift in engineered systems. it allows researchers to test the robustness of synthetic organisms under diverse environmental conditions. BioPlayground provides a powerful platform for directed evolution and ecological modeling, helping researchers understand how functions are maintained.
