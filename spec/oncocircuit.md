# OncoCircuit

OncoCircuit: A design platform for synthetic gene circuits that specifically target and eliminate cancer cells. It identifies tumor-specific promoters and designs logic gates that trigger therapeutic responses only in malignant tissue. OncoCircuit provides detailed molecular mechanisms for targeted interventions, offering a new frontier in highly selective oncology treatments.
