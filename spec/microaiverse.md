# MicroAiverse

MicroAiverse: An AI solver for the "plate count anomaly," suggesting media recipes and co-culture partners to cultivate previously unculturable microbes. It uses metabolic flux data to identify required nutrients and symbiotic relationships. MicroAiverse enables the exploration of microbial diversity, unlocking new potential for the discovery of natural products.
