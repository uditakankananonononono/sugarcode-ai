# Organoid AI

Organoid AI: An AI-driven platform for designing and simulating patient-derived organoid models. It predicts optimal growth conditions and suggests media recipes while mapping spatial gene expression. Organoid AI creates digital twins of 3D tissues to simulate growth and drug response, providing an accurate model for studying disease biology.
