# Cell-Free Opt

Cell-Free Opt: An AI optimizer for cell-free protein synthesis (CFPS) systems, suggesting ideal reagent concentrations to maximize protein yield. It simulates reaction kinetics and calculates costs for specific experimental setups. Cell-Free Opt enables rapid prototyping of genetic designs without the limitations of living cells, providing a fast evaluation platform.
