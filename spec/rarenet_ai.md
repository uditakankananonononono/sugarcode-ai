# RareNet AI

RareNet AI: A diagnostic reasoning tool for rare diseases that correlates patient symptoms with genomic and multi-omic data. It identifies candidate diseases and associated genes with high confidence scores. RareNet AI uses an explainable framework to help clinicians navigate the complexity of rare conditions, providing insights into treatments.
