# Tissue Eng

Tissue Eng: A design tool for 3D bioprinting strategies, suggesting biomaterials and cell types for creating vascularized tissue models. It calibrates printing parameters for scaffolds and simulates mechanical responses under physiological stress. Tissue Eng ensures structural fidelity and functional viability, providing a platform for developing advanced models for drug testing.
