# Stability AI

Stability AI: A module that forecasts the genetic and phenotypic stability of engineered systems, identifying failure modes like plasmid loss. It provides stability scores and simulates mutational drift over multiple cell generations. Stability AI helps researchers design robust systems that maintain intended functions during long-term storage or large-scale fermentation.
