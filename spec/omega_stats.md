# Omega Stats

Omega Stats: A performance dashboard for the SugarCode AI platform, tracking prediction accuracy, efficiency rates, and throughput across sub-networks. It provides a global view of the system's precision, from chemical scores to clinical simulation times. Omega Stats ensures transparency and reliability, allowing users to trust AI-guided biological engineering.
