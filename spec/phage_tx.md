# Phage-Tx

Phage-Tx: A predictive model for phage therapy efficacy that simulates the coevolutionary arms race between bacteriophages and their bacterial hosts. It suggests optimal phage cocktails for targeting resistant strains and identifies modifications. Phage-Tx provides a dynamic view of outcomes, helping researchers design effective and resilient phage-based treatments.
