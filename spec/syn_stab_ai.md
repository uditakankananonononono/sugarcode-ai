# Syn-Stab AI

Syn-Stab AI: A predictive tool that evaluates the evolutionary stability of synthetic gene circuits and host organisms. It forecasts circuit failure risk due to metabolic burden or mutational drift over generations. Syn-Stab AI suggests genetic modifications to increase functional half-life, preventing the loss of synthetic functions during industrial processes.

Syn-Stab AI is a predictive platform for evaluating and optimizing the long-term stability of engineered biological systems, focusing on how synthetic constructs behave under evolutionary pressures and metabolic constraints. It models genetic circuits and pathways as dynamic systems subject to mutation, selection, and drift, predicting how their functionality degrades or adapts over successive generations. The system uses probabilistic models and evolutionary simulations to estimate failure rates and identify weak points in design.
At the molecular level, Syn-Stab AI analyzes sequence features that influence mutation rates and structural stability, such as repetitive elements, GC content, and secondary structures. It predicts hotspots for recombination, insertion, or deletion events that could disrupt synthetic constructs. It also evaluates protein stability and folding robustness, identifying mutations that may compromise function or lead to aggregation.
From a systems biology perspective, the platform models the interaction between synthetic constructs and host cellular networks. It quantifies metabolic burden, resource competition (ribosomes, ATP, cofactors), and regulatory interference, all of which can drive selective pressure against engineered systems. Using fitness landscape modeling, it predicts which mutations are likely to be favored under given environmental conditions, enabling proactive design modifications to mitigate evolutionary loss of function.
Chemically, Syn-Stab AI considers the stability of expressed molecules and intermediates, including degradation pathways and reactive byproducts that may influence cellular fitness. It models how chemical stress—oxidative damage, pH fluctuations—affects both the host and the synthetic system, integrating these factors into its stability predictions.
The output includes stability scores, predicted failure modes, and recommended design modifications, such as codon optimization, circuit redundancy, or genomic integration strategies. By combining evolutionary biology, molecular genetics, and systems modeling, Syn-Stab AI ensures that engineered biological systems remain functional over time, making it a critical component for industrial-scale synthetic biology and long-term deployments.
