# Organoid Screen

Organoid Screen: A virtual drug screening platform that tests compounds on patient-derived organoid models. It predicts clinical efficacy and identifies biomarkers associated with drug response. Organoid Screen visualizes interaction networks and calculates synergy scores, helping researchers identify the most effective and personalized treatment strategies using 3D biological models.

Organoid Screen
Organoid Screen is a computational drug discovery and personalized medicine platform that constructs high-resolution digital organoid models from patient-derived multi-omics and imaging datasets. It integrates single-cell RNA sequencing, spatial transcriptomics, proteomics, and histopathology into unified tissue atlases that preserve both cellular heterogeneity and spatial organization. Each organoid is represented as a spatially resolved cellular graph where nodes correspond to individual cells and edges represent biochemical, mechanical, and paracrine signaling interactions.
At the cellular simulation layer, each cell is modeled as an autonomous computational agent governed by gene regulatory networks, metabolic flux constraints, and signaling pathway dynamics. Cellular state transitions such as proliferation, apoptosis, differentiation, and senescence are governed by stochastic hybrid systems combining deterministic ODE signaling models with probabilistic switching functions. Microenvironmental coupling is achieved through diffusion-reaction equations governing oxygen gradients, nutrient availability, cytokine signaling, and drug diffusion across tissue compartments.
Drug interaction modeling integrates pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics within the organoid environment. Drug molecules are simulated as diffusing chemical agents with binding kinetics governed by receptor occupancy models and enzymatic degradation pathways. Intracellular drug effects are propagated through signaling network perturbation models that simulate pathway inhibition, feedback activation, and resistance mechanism emergence. This enables prediction of both acute cytotoxic effects and long-term adaptive resistance evolution.
Synergistic drug effects are computed using combinatorial perturbation analysis across nonlinear signaling networks. The system evaluates synergy using multiple mechanistic criteria including pathway convergence, synthetic lethality interactions, and compensatory feedback suppression. Machine learning models trained on high-throughput drug screening datasets refine these predictions by learning latent interaction structures beyond known biological pathways.
Finally, Organoid Screen outputs patient-specific therapeutic strategies ranked by predicted efficacy, toxicity risk, and resistance likelihood. It generates biomarker signatures associated with response states, including gene expression patterns, pathway activation states, and spatial tumor microenvironment features. This enables fully virtual clinical trials where drug combinations are tested in silico before experimental validation.
